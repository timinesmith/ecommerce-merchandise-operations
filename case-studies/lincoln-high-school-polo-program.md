# Lincoln High School Polo Proposal — Quoting & Costing Case Study

> **Correction note (Oct 31, 2026, per Timi):** This was a quote that did not go through; the earlier version of this case study wrongly framed it as a completed program. The proposal did NOT proceed to production or delivery. All figures below are QUOTED figures from the proposal.

## Client need

Lincoln High School, within the School District of Philadelphia, sought pricing for 2,500 uniform polos to be delivered in phases against the school calendar. The district's payment process pays 30 days after delivery through its Oracle system, making production sequencing and working-capital planning part of the quoting problem.

## Timothy's role

Founder & Owner, PhillyScreen LLC — quoting, costing, phased-delivery planning, and supplier pricing in 2022.

## Approach (proposal stage)

1. Structured the proposed order as four phased deliveries with separate due dates.
2. Built the quote economics before any production: sales price, cost of goods, and planned margin.
3. Prepared per-phase invoicing, beginning with a part 1 of 4 invoice at proposal stage.
4. Incorporated net-30 post-delivery payment timing into the proposed production plan.
5. Coordinated supplier pricing and a proposed delivery schedule as the single accountable operator.

## Outcome

**The quote did not go through; the proposal did not proceed to production.** The case is retained in this repository strictly as evidence of institutional quoting discipline: unit-level costing, a four-phase delivery structure, aligned invoicing, and payment-terms planning for a public-school procurement process.
