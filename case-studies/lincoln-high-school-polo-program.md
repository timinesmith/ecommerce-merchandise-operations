# Lincoln High School Polo Program — Case Study

## Client need

Lincoln High School, within the School District of Philadelphia, needed 2,500 uniform polos delivered in phases against the school calendar. The district's payment process paid 30 days after delivery through its Oracle system, making production sequencing and working-capital planning part of the operating problem.

## Timothy's role

Founder & Owner, PhillyScreen LLC — quoting, costing, production scheduling, supplier coordination, invoicing, and delivery management in 2022.

## Approach

1. Structured the order as four phased deliveries with separate due dates.
2. Built the order economics before production: sales, cost of goods, and margin.
3. Issued per-phase invoicing, beginning with part 1 of 4 at order start.
4. Incorporated net-30 post-delivery payment timing into the production plan.
5. Coordinated production start, supplier costs, and delivery scheduling as the single accountable operator.

## Documented economics

| Measure | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Units | 2,500 polos |

| Unit margin | $5.50 |

## Result

The four-phase structure created a repeatable model for institutional uniform programs: clear unit economics, staged production, aligned invoicing, and delivery accountability.
