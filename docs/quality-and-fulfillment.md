# Quality and Fulfillment Controls

**Status:** Illustrative operating framework.

## Pre-production

- Approved artwork and product specification are locked before purchasing.
- Size run and quantities are reconciled to the order total.
- Delivery date is tested against supplier lead time and production capacity.

## In production

- First-piece check compares garment, decoration placement, color, and size.
- Exceptions are photographed or logged for internal review where permitted.
- Phase completion is recorded only after count verification.

## Before delivery

- Count by size and variant.
- Inspect a sample from each size group.
- Pack by phase or destination to reduce sorting at delivery.
- Match packing list, invoice phase, and delivery confirmation.

## After delivery

- Record shortages, defects, or customer corrections.
- Close the phase only when delivery and invoicing status are both known.
- Capture lessons for the next phase before production begins.
